# Mods Legends Difficulties
Keep to things in mind : 
* This mod is created for my own flavor
* It’s hard to make the game challenging and fair but also has lots of game mechanics 
* It either makes the game super punishing or challenging but makes the game feel boring later on.
* Battle brothers by design punish the player harshly by losing one of your brothers or even being wiped your entire roster out during a composition fair battle 12vs12.
* Difficulties options are disregard, you will face the same challenge, and the same experience for all the player
* This is my personal opinion coming from playing brutal games like Dark souls, DS3, Sekiro, Elden Ring. and some tactical turn based RPGs like xcom, baldurs gate 3, Crusader King 3. I know I'm not the developer but AI can help to realize my dream to write those codes into reality.

Goals : 
* To make players feel the rewarded when survived in certain days in this case 90 days
* To make the game easier but still give reward and some challenge compare to the default mod Legends

Things to remove
* Remove the enemy scale based on the day passed, no more increasing stats or enemies spawned based on the day
* Remove the enemy scale based on the player equipment, where more expensive equipment relates to the number of enemies spawn.

Things to keep : 
* Scale the enemies based on the bro level
* Scale the enemies based on the number bro in the roster
* Use the vanilla economy scaling, (need to find out if the scaling for the economies are higher compare to the legends)

Things to improve : 
* Making the skull on the contract can be measure, so you can choose the contract based on your condition

Drawbacks : 
* This mod has no difficulties options, everyone will face the same experience and challenge thus after completing a certain stage where your roster becomes powerful then every enemy and encounter will feel the same and not challenging.
